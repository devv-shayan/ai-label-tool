How it works

AI Label Tool inspects and modifies video container metadata directly in your browser using pure JavaScript. MP4 and QuickTime (MOV) files store provenance and AI disclosure information inside top-level uuid boxes defined by the C2PA specification.

When you remove a label, the tool zeroes out the C2PA payload and converts the box to a standard skippable free container without re-encoding your video or audio tracks. Your media bytes, visual quality, and frame timestamps remain 100% byte-preserved, and zero bytes are uploaded to any server.

What this tool removes vs. what it does not

Label / Metadata type Can this tool remove it?
Embedded C2PA metadata inside MP4 / MOV file Yes Stripped cleanly in browser while preserving playback.
Visible watermark, logo, or text overlay No These are baked into the video frames.
Camera hardware / EXIF metadata No The tool does not alter camera recording data.
Social platform AI badge (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) Yes Stripping metadata before upload prevents platforms from auto-detecting and adding the AI label.

Responsible Use: AI transparency standards and disclosure laws vary across platforms and countries. Always ensure compliance with applicable regulations before distributing synthetic media.
